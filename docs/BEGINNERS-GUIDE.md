# Beginners guide

This guide is for everyone, even if you have never done anything like this before. Take your time. Read one step, do it, then read the next one. You cannot hurt anything by reading.

## What is Hearth?

Your LG TV has a home screen. It is busy: it has ads, big pictures and many boxes. **Hearth is a different home screen.** It is calm. It shows the time, the weather and a row of your apps, like a shelf with your favourite things on it.

Hearth is an **app**, like YouTube or any other app on your TV. You install it once, and then you can use it every day.

## Two ways to use Hearth

Think of a TV as a house. Normally LG owns the house and you can only use the rooms LG opens for you.

1. **Not rooted (only Developer Mode).** You get a key to the front room. Hearth works, but you open it yourself, like opening a book.
2. **Rooted.** You get a key to the whole house. Hearth can then open by itself when you press **Home** or switch the TV on, and it can do many more things.

"Rooting" sounds scary, but it only means "getting the big key". It carries a small risk, so read the warnings in [Step 2](guide/rooting.md) before you decide. **You do not have to root your TV.** Hearth works without it.

## Words explained

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **webOS** | The brain of your LG TV. It is the system that runs all the apps. |
| **Firmware** | The TV's own software, with a number such as `04.60.90`. Updates change it. |
| **Developer Mode** | A free LG app that lets you put your own apps on the TV. |
| **Root / rooting** | Getting the "big key" for the TV, so apps may do more. |
| **Homebrew Channel** | An app store for people who like to tinker. It appears on the TV after rooting. |
| **IP address** | The TV's house number on your home network, for example `192.168.1.20`. |
| **SSH** | A way to type a message to the TV from a computer. You can do most things without it. |
| **IPK file** | The installer of a TV app. Like a zip file for the TV. |
| **Wi-Fi network** | The invisible web that connects your TV, phone and computer. They must be on the same one. |

## Before you start

- **Check that the TV is yours**, or that its owner agrees.
- You need: your LG TV, the TV remote, a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) or an Android phone, and about an hour. The TV and the computer or phone must be on the **same Wi-Fi**.
- **Do not update the TV's software before rooting**, and turn off automatic updates. Some updates close the door for good, so rooting stops working. You can check your TV at <https://cani.rootmy.tv> first.
- **Write down three things** about your TV: the model, the webOS version and the firmware. Find them in **Settings, Support, TV Information**. You will need them to choose the right way to root and to tell us if Hearth works.
- **Give the TV a fixed IP address** in your router or in the TV's network settings, so its "house number" does not change. Hearth also shows the number at the bottom right of its Settings screen.
