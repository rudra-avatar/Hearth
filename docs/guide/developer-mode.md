# Step 1: Developer Mode

Developer Mode is a free LG app. It gives you the first key: you can put your own apps on the TV. You need it to install Hearth if the TV is not rooted. It does not change anything dangerous.

## Do this

1. On a computer or phone, make a free **LG developer account** at <https://webostv.developer.lge.com/>. LG sends you an e-mail; click the link in it.
2. On the TV open the **LG Content Store** (the shopping bag app) and install the app called **Developer Mode**.
3. Open it and sign in with the account you just made.
4. Switch **Dev Mode Status** to **On**. The TV restarts by itself.
5. Open the app again. Check that it says **ON**. Then switch **Key Server** to **On**. Keep this screen open while you do the next step.

## Good to know

- Developer Mode **runs out after 1000 hours**. Open the app now and then and press the extend button, like winding up a clock. When it runs out, the apps you added are removed. A rooted TV can wind it up by itself.
- If your computer can see the TV but cannot add it, the TV's **User agreements** may be in the way. Go to **Settings, General, User agreements**, untick them (the TV restarts), open Developer Mode again and switch **Key Server** on.

Help from the community: <https://www.webosbrew.org/devmode>.
