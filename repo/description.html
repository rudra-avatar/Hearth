Hearth for WebOS is a fast, calm home screen for LG webOS TVs. It works from webOS 3 up to the newest versions (webOS 25/26) and is designed for speed: it shows at once and stays smooth, even on slow TVs.

Your clock, date and weather sit on a plain, picture or slideshow background, with your apps and TV inputs in one row along the bottom.

Home screen

  • Your apps and inputs: choose what shows, re-order, hide, uninstall, give your own names and icons (or a picture from a USB drive).
  • Favourites: a short row of your most used apps; Down shows all the others.
  • Order by last opened and a Recent apps panel on Back (rooted).
  • Clock, date and weather: 27 fonts, any position, size, colour and opacity; weather by city search or a list of cities, no account needed.
  • Quiet mode and OLED care: after a pause only the clock stays, the screen dims, and the picture shifts a little to protect OLED panels.

Looks

  • Six dark themes, seven icon styles (original, minimal, mono, soft, accent, gradient, tinted tile) with dark or light tiles, icon colour and opacity.
  • 25 bundled photos, your own photo, or a slideshow of a whole folder with shuffle and interval.
  • Custom themes (rooted): load a zip with colours, fonts, icons, app order, names, cities and a wallpaper, or export your apps as a starting point.
  • Languages: Indonesian, Spanish, German and Arabic (machine translated, waiting for native speakers) or your own language file.

On a rooted TV

  • Home button opens Hearth: Hearth bundles the Input Hook key hook and starts it itself, so no other app is needed. A helper script also covers leaving apps with Back.
  • TV start: the TV can open Hearth, or an app of your choice, when you switch it on.
  • Remote buttons (experimental): give the colour buttons and other dedicated buttons a new job.
  • Screen saver (experimental): your pictures, GIFs, videos or a slideshow, or a built-in fireplace video, in place of the TV's saver; it can switch the TV off after a chosen time.
  • Memory: see which LG apps stay loaded in the background and switch them off; Keep Hearth ready makes it open faster.

Not rooted

The home screen, clock, weather, themes, photos and launching apps work. The full app list loads through LG Connect Apps (tested on webOS 11.2); Quick Access 1-9 opens Hearth from any screen. Options that need root are greyed out with the reason.

Reference device: LG firmware 04.60.90 (webOS 7.6.0). Parts not tested on a TV are marked experimental, for example the screen saver on webOS 10 and newer.
